Hi! I am an undergraduate student at Tongji University, currently an undergraduate visiting student at UC Berkeley, working with Prof. Koushil Sreenath in the Hybrid Robotics Group (HRG). I am grateful to Prof. Sreenath for the opportunity and guidance, and to all members of HRG for their generous help.

Previously, I was fortunate to work with Prof. Junqiao Zhao at the TiEV Lab, Tongji University, on meta-reinforcement learning, and with Prof. Hang Su at the THU-ML Lab, Tsinghua University, on robotic manipulation. I am deeply grateful to both of them for their mentorship.

My research focuses on learning-based control for humanoid robots. I am particularly interested in:

  • Reinforcement learning for whole-body control, especially dynamic, contact-rich skills;
  • Object interaction and loco-manipulation, such as dribbling a ball at high speed;
  • Sim-to-real transfer, bridging simulation and real hardware with onboard perception.
